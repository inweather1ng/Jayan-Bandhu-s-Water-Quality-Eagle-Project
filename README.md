# Jayan-Bandhu's-Water-Quality-Eagle-Project
This is an Eagle project that aims to track the creek health of the Dry Creek Watershed using eDNA and water chemistry measurements. Physiochemical measurements will be conducted on a monthly basis 

This eagle project contains two main components, eDNA and physiochemical. 

eDNA types:
1. Monthly Metabarcoding of BMI will be conducted to understand how the presence of pollution sensitive organisms is affected by characteristics of the two creeks. Samples are collected as a part of a Boy Scout eagle project.
2. qPCR assays are measured quantitatively modeling the spread of two potent invasive species: New Zealand Mudsnails and Asian clams.

All eDNA data will be shared on eDNA explorer, and processed by the CALeDNA lab at UCSC for metabarcoding. Furthermore, All qPCR assays will be analyzed by the Jonah Ventures laboratory. 

Physiochemical monitoring will be conducted monthly, and the target parameters are Conductivity, PH, Dissolved Oxygen, Temperature, and Turbidity. All equipment is being loaned from the Sierra Streams institute.
